IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
================================================================================

Successfully implemented two new advanced stereoscopic rendering modes:

1. STEREO_POLARIZED (Mode ID: 6)
   - Scanline-based polarized stereoscopy
   - Encodes left/right images on alternating scanlines
   - Full horizontal resolution per eye
   - Requires polarized 3D display + passive glasses

2. STEREO_CHECKERBOARD (Mode ID: 7)
   - Pixel-level checkerboard pattern interlacing
   - Left eye on black squares, right on white
   - ~50% effective resolution per eye
   - Works with parallax barrier or autostereoscopic displays

================================================================================
SHADER IMPLEMENTATIONS
================================================================================

POLARIZED SHADER:
  - Uses gl_FragCoord.y for scanline detection
  - Even scanlines (0,2,4...): Left eye texture
  - Odd scanlines (1,3,5...): Right eye texture
  - GLSL 1.2 compatible
  - Full color preservation

CHECKERBOARD SHADER:
  - Uses gl_FragCoord.x + gl_FragCoord.y for pattern detection
  - Alternating pixels create checkerboard pattern
  - (x+y) even: Left eye
  - (x+y) odd: Right eye
  - GLSL 1.2 compatible
  - Pixel-level interlacing

HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

POLARIZED MODE:
  - Display: Polarized 3D LCD/LED (100+ Hz recommended)
  - Glasses: Passive polarized 3D (cinema-style)
  - Resolution: 1080p or higher for quality
  - Graphics API: OpenGL 1.2+

CHECKERBOARD MODE:
  - Display: Parallax barrier OR autostereoscopic lenticular
  - Viewing: Passive (no glasses needed for parallax barrier)
  - Resolution: Any (optimized 1080p+)
  - Graphics API: OpenGL 1.2+
